# About OpenNotes

**OpenNotes** is a community-based open-source platform dedicated to the free and open exchange of educational resources. We believe no education should be locked, paywalled, or restricted.

### Our Mission
Our mission is to spread knowledge as a resource available to everyone.
And the goal is simple: **To make notes open.** 

Whether you're a student or educator, this platform is intended to be a central hub of high-quality community-oriented knowledge to support your personal growth and to share with others too.

### How It Works
OpenNotes is grounded in a "Serverless" architecture, built with modern web technologies to ensure a transparent, inclusive, and secure experience:

*   **Open Source:** The platform's core front-end code is visible on GitHub. Our belief lies in the transparency of not just content and education, but a clearness that extends to how our platform operates.
*   **Community Driven:** Content is powered by students and users like you. We believe in the fundamental of inclusivity in education where everyone has equal access to use our platform and contribute to our goals.
*   **Safety First:** Our site utilizes Cloudflare's secure human verification and automatically detects for malicious uploads in order to ensure a secure and safe experience for everyone.

### Contribute
The best way to support OpenNotes and our initiative to make education open is through contributing notes and content. We encourage you to continue our initiative by uploading your own high-quality and educational notes to cultivate growth within other aspiring students and educators.
